Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The Spunk app is provided by Albert Medya. This policy explains what data we process, why, and your rights, in line with Turkey’s KVKK (Law No. 6698) and the GDPR.

Data we collect

• Account: your name and email when you sign in with Apple or Google; a device-specific ID when you continue as a guest.
• Phone number: only if you choose to verify it, for reward delivery and account security.
• App usage: your predictions, picks, streak and task progress, gem balance and the rewards you get.
• Notifications: if you allow them, your device’s push token and your notification preferences.

Why we use it

To create and protect your account, settle your predictions and award gems, calculate the leaderboard, deliver your rewards, send the notifications you choose and keep the service secure.

Sharing

We don’t sell your data or share it for advertising. We only share it with providers needed to run the service (Apple and Google for sign-in, server hosting, push and SMS verification services) and with authorities when the law requires it. Your username and score are visible to other players on the leaderboard.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted or anonymized, subject to legal retention duties.

Your rights

You can access, correct and delete your data and object to its processing. You can permanently delete your account at any time in My account › Delete account. For other requests, contact us through the Help center.

Children

Spunk isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy and will announce important changes in the app.

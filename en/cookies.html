Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Do we use cookies?

Spunk is a mobile app and doesn’t use browser cookies. We also don’t use advertising or third-party tracking tools.

What we store on your device

• Session keys: in the device’s secure storage, to keep you signed in.
• Guest device ID: to bring your guest account back on this device.
• Preferences: your appearance and language choices.

Deleting it

Session keys are deleted when you sign out. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.
